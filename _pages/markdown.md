## About This Page

This page is now a lightweight maintenance note for the lab website.

## Main Editing Locations

- Site-wide settings: `_config.yml`
- Top navigation: `_data/navigation.yml`
- Home page: `_pages/about.md`
- Lab pages: `_pages/people.md`, `_pages/research.md`, `_pages/contact.md`
- Publications: `_publications/`
- News posts: `_posts/`
- Static PDFs and supporting files: `files/`
- Images: `images/`
- Photos: `photos/`
- Research figures: `research/`

## Suggested Next Updates

- Add new publications and related links
- Update news posts for papers, recruiting, and lab milestones
- Continue expanding People and Research content as the lab grows
