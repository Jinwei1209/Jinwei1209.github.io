## Join the Lab

The Intelligent MRI Lab at the University of Massachusetts Amherst is currently being established. We welcome inquiries from prospective PhD students, postdocs, and collaborators who are excited about quantitative MRI, computational imaging, machine learning, and translational neuroimaging.

## Areas of Interest

- MRI reconstruction and inverse problems
- Quantitative MRI
- Quantitative susceptibility mapping
- Lesion analysis and longitudinal neuroimaging
- Machine learning for medical imaging

## How to Reach Out

If you are interested in joining the lab, please email [jinweizhang@umass.edu](mailto:jinweizhang@umass.edu) with a short introduction, your CV, and a brief note about your research interests.

## Current Status

Formal openings, project descriptions, and recruiting updates will be posted here as the lab continues to grow.
