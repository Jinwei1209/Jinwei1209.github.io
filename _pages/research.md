## Overview

The lab develops methods that combine quantitative MRI, inverse problems, computer vision, and clinically motivated neuroimaging analysis.

## Current Directions

### Accelerated MRI

Fast acquisition and reconstruction methods for quantitative imaging, with an emphasis on practical deployment and robust performance.

### Quantitative Susceptibility Mapping

Methods for susceptibility estimation, reconstruction, and downstream analysis in neurological applications.

### Lesion Analysis

Automated segmentation, longitudinal tracking, and biomarker discovery for white matter lesion burden and disease progression.

### AI for Neuroimaging

Machine learning models for image analysis, harmonization, prediction, and decision support in MRI-driven studies.
