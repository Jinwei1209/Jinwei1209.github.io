# Intelligent MRI Lab

The Intelligent MRI Lab develops artificial intelligence and computational MRI methods for quantitative neuroimaging, accelerated MRI acquisition, quantitative susceptibility mapping (QSM), and white matter lesion analysis.

Current research focuses include:
- Accelerated quantitative MRI
- Quantitative susceptibility mapping
- Multiple sclerosis lesion analysis
- Longitudinal lesion tracking
- AI for computational neuroimaging

We are recruiting motivated PhD students and postdocs.
