Mission
======
The Intelligent MRI Lab develops AI and computational imaging methods for quantitative MRI, neuroimaging analysis, and clinically relevant imaging biomarkers.

Research Focus
======
* Accelerated MRI acquisition and reconstruction
* Quantitative tissue parameter mapping
* White matter lesion analysis
* Longitudinal neuroimaging biomarkers
* Machine learning for computational MRI

Open Positions
======
We welcome inquiries from prospective PhD students, postdocs, and research collaborators interested in MRI, inverse problems, and translational AI.
