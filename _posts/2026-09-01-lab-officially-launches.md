The Intelligent MRI Lab will officially launch at the University of Massachusetts Amherst on September 1, 2026.

We are excited to begin building the lab's research program in quantitative MRI, computational imaging, neuroimaging analysis, and AI for clinically relevant imaging problems.

More updates on research, recruiting, and lab activities will be posted here as the lab gets underway.
